# International Trade Dashboard

A simple website for exploring trade between countries. Pick two countries, a range of years and a product category, and see their imports and exports over time on a chart.

Available in English and Hebrew, with light and dark mode.

Data comes from the [UN Comtrade](https://comtradeplus.un.org/) database.

Developed for Dr. Erez Shoshani's *Israel's Foreign Policy* course assignment, Ruppin Academic Center.
